What you get

Two parts, one price. The code gets you running. The lessons make it yours.

What it isn't

  • No stock tips, no signals, no group to copy trades from.
  • No promised returns. A system can be built well and still lose money.
  • Not my picks. The default settings are a starting point, not a recommendation for you; the rules and what it trades are your decision.
  • Never holds your money. It stays in your own Trading 212 ISA, in your name.

Your copy is yours

You can read every line, change anything and run it as your own. What you can't do is pass it on, so here's how it works.

  • Delivered by private invite. After you buy, you're invited to the private code repo on GitHub. There's no zip file to forward.
  • Every copy is marked. Each buyer's copy carries its own unique ID, so a shared or posted copy traces straight back to whoever shared it.
  • A personal licence. For your own use only: no sharing, reselling or publishing. Breaking it ends your updates and support.
  • 12 months of updates included. Fixes, new brokers and improvements. After that it's £99 a year if you want them, and it never renews on its own. How updates work
  • Shared copies go stale. No updates, no lessons, no support and no community. Copies posted online are taken down.
