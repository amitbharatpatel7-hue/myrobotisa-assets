              Set up my robot

              About ten minutes, and no typing into GitHub. You bring two keys; this page builds your copy, locks the keys away, switches on the schedule and runs the setup check. Then you watch it work on paper money, and the lessons explain what you just built.

              🔒Your keys never touch our servers. They go from this page straight into your own GitHub, locked with GitHub's encryption so only your copy of the code can read them. We can't see them. The GitHub connection is used for this setup and forgotten when you close the page.
              1. Connect GitHubTo do

                GitHub is the free website that keeps your copy of the code and runs it on a schedule. No account yet? Make one free with this email, then come back.

              2. Your Trading 212 practice keyTo do

                Practice account first: no real money until Lesson 13.

                Where do I find this?
                1. Open Trading 212, tap your account at the top and choose Switch to Practice.
                2. Settings → API (Beta) → Generate key. Tick Account data, Portfolio, Metadata, History, Orders and Pies.
                3. It shows a key and a secret once. Copy each one straight into the boxes below.
              3. Your email keyTo do

                So your robot can email you its plan and how it's doing.

                Where do I find this?
                1. Sign up free at resend.com with the inbox you want the emails in, and confirm the email it sends.
                2. API Keys → Create. Sending access is enough. Copy the key (it starts with re_).

                Must be the address you signed up to Resend with.
