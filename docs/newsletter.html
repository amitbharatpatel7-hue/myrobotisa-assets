Sample issue

Eleven rejected orders and one decimal place

The L. Monday morning, eleven of the robot's orders bounced. Not one filled. The log said "invalid quantity" eleven times and nothing else. The money sat in cash for a week while we worked out why.

The lesson. We were sending quantities to six decimal places. Trading 212 accepts four. The fix was one line; the real fix was a check that rounds every order to what the exchange accepts before it's sent, and a log line that says what was sent, not just that it failed. That check is now Day 7 of the lessons.

What's new. The site moved to separate pages. The demo has a QuickStart tab. The welcome clip is live on the home page.

    Visitor counts

    We count visits to this site without cookies and without storing anything that identifies you: each visit is turned into a scrambled code from your IP address and browser, mixed with a random value that is replaced every day, so we can count unique visitors per day but never follow anyone from one day to the next. We also note the page, the site that linked here and whether it's a phone or a computer. Visits sent with "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" switched on aren't counted.
